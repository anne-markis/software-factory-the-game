A3 click-to-zoom — the strip stays hidden until you ask

A3 (boxed cycle) stays the spine: six boxes, a small cycle inside In Progress, one rework leak on the Shipped → Ready U. The contributor strip is no longer always on. Zoomable stages (In Progress, Done) carry a caret so later Ready / Shipped zooms can use the same move. Try each variant — they start collapsed.

T1 · Hang — click the box, panel under that stage

Click In Progress or Done. A dashed panel opens under that stage (wider than the box, still anchored to it). Click again to close; click the other stage to switch. Ideas / Plan / Ready / Shipped have no caret yet.

Try: click In Progress, then Done, then Done again.

Delivery

What to notice. Collapsed, you only watch the factory line — same as today’s Delivery, plus a cycle glyph and carets. Open, the zoom is still “of this box.” A long hire list will outgrow a hang centered on one column.

T2 · Drawer — click the box, full-width band

Same click-the-stage rule, but the open panel spans the whole spine. The heading names the stage. Better room for contributors, and every future stage zoom is the same shape.

Try: click In Progress, then Done — the drawer swaps content, it does not stack.

Delivery

What to notice. Extensibility is free: a Ready or Shipped zoom is another drawer payload, not a new layout. Cost: the open panel is not visually “under” the box you clicked except for the thicker border and caret.

T3 · Caret only — the box is for watching

The stage box itself is not a button. Only the ▾ in the corner opens the full-width drawer. Clicking the rest of In Progress does nothing. Safer at 5x when you are staring at the loop.

Try: click the In Progress numbers (nothing), then the ▾, then Done’s ▾.

Delivery

What to notice. Matches “only visible if a user clicks on something” most literally. The caret is a small target. Pair with the caption, or this variant becomes invisible.

T4 · Peek chip — name the zoom before it opens

Collapsed boxes that have a zoom show a chip: “3 drivers ▾” / “why bound ▾”. The chip is the hit target; the box stays watch-only. Drawer is full-width, same as T2.

Try: click “3 drivers ▾” under In Progress, then “why bound ▾” under Done.

Delivery

What to notice. Best discoverability of the four, and it scales: a later Ready zoom can say “pull drivers ▾” without teaching a new control. Cost: extra chrome on the spine even when collapsed — two chips, still much quieter than today’s always-on Progress panel.
